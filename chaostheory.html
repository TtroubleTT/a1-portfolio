Overview

At Chaos Theory I am the Founder and Project Manager that makes sure everything runs smoothly. I manage the many different teams that I have set up to delegate tasks to like: the moderation team responsible for keeping the servers safe, the QA Testers which test upcoming plugin updates and confirm bugs, the Wiki Team that keeps information on our wiki up to date, the Content Team who creates trailers, clip complications, and guides, and most importantly the development team which is responsible for maintaining and creating new plugins for the server. The development team is the team I am most hands on where I lead creating new experiences for players and review the code quality made by other team members. I am also responsible for organizing tasks for my team to do and explaining them in ways that are easy for them to break apart.

Chaos Theory has an active player base of 20,000+ users making it one of the most popular SCPSL servers. Our discord server has 12,000+ people in it. We are featured in many popular content creatures videos and are fully self funded and profitable.

14.1 Tooth And Nail

The 14.1 Tooth And Nail update was the first update that I was there for the entire development cycle. I worked a lot in supporting developers in the major additions that were SCP-127, and the new keycards. One of the main things I implemented was the SCP Opt out setting that lets players choose to not play as an SCP unless there is nobody else who can. I made sure to implement it in a way that wouldn't softlock lobbies without an SCP and in a way that wasn't abusable by players to try to game the system to get SCP.

14.2 Songs Of Ash

The 14.2 Songs of Ash update featured some systems changes done by my. First was a refractor of the item spawning system to give Game Design better control of what items can spawn, their groupings, and the chances that they can spawn. I also implemented Counter Miniwaves which was an extension of the Miniwave feature that when a wave failed to meat requirements it would spawn a counter wave. I had to make sure the information was properly displayed in the UI and I made it easily updatable for potential visual indicators that Game Design may want later.
